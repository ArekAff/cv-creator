## Original posting (verbatim)

**Senior Data Analyst — Network Planning**

Vantaro Logistics moves 30 million parcels a year through 12 sortation hubs in Central Europe. Our
Network Planning team decides how many people, trucks and sort lines each hub needs, every week.
We are looking for a Senior Data Analyst who turns operational data into decisions our hub managers
trust.

What you will do
- Build and own forecasts of parcel volumes per hub and per day, and explain them to operations.
- Design self-service dashboards in Power BI for hub managers and shift leaders.
- Model data in our warehouse (BigQuery + dbt) so that reporting is reliable and tested.
- Work side by side with operations teams to understand how decisions are really made on the floor.
- Mentor two junior analysts.

What you bring
- 4+ years of experience in data analytics, ideally in logistics, supply chain or retail.
- Advanced SQL. You are comfortable with window functions and data modelling.
- Python for analysis and forecasting (pandas, scikit-learn or statsmodels).
- Power BI (or Tableau) at an advanced level.
- Experience with dbt or a similar transformation tool.
- Strong stakeholder management with non-technical, operational teams.
- Very good English; Polish is a plus.

Nice to have
- Google Cloud Platform / BigQuery.
- Airflow or another orchestration tool.
- Experience mentoring or training others.

We value ownership, clarity and calm under pressure — peak season is real.
