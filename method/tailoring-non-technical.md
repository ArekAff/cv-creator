## The principle
- **N-1 Prove results in the ad's own terms.** Screeners look for the ad's metrics, the ad's
  vocabulary, and evidence for each soft skill it names. [Non-technical overview]
- **N-15 A generic CV is about you; a tailored CV is about them.** Every line should answer "does
  this prove I can do this job?". [Resume job description]

## By family
- **N-2 Sales: the numbers are the evidence.**
  - Show quota attainment (% of target), rank in the team (#2 of 15), revenue, pipeline, deal size,
    new logos and awards, like the stats on a baseball card.
  - Mirror the ad's sales vocabulary: if it says "hunter" three times, the word belongs on the CV.
  - Make client-facing and cold-calling experience visible when the ad asks.
  - At start-ups, a short "Activities & Interests" line can show culture fit.

  [Sales resume; Non-technical overview]
- **N-3 Marketing: match the KPIs, not just the duties.** Traffic, conversion, CPL/CAC, engagement,
  pipeline sourced, ROAS. Name the tools: HubSpot, GA4, CMS, Salesforce, ad platforms.
  [Non-technical overview; CAR guide]
- **N-4 HR & recruiting: a specialist, not a generalist, and not an autobiography.**
  - Mirror the HR scope the ad describes: talent acquisition, employee relations, L&D, HRIS,
    compliance.
  - Show, don't tell, with team sizes, hires made, time-to-fill and retention.
  - Values words from the company description ("think critically, move quickly") are keywords too.

  [HR 5 tips; Non-technical overview]
- **N-5 Admin & customer service: every soft skill gets a bullet that proves it.** For example,
  organisation becomes "managed calendars, travel and expense reports for 5 executives". Measure
  volume, speed, satisfaction and accuracy: tickets per day, CSAT, response time, error rate. A soft
  skill without a supporting bullet is filler. [Non-technical overview; CAR guide; Resume job
  description]
- **N-6 Business roles at tech companies** (product, operations, customer success): surface
  experience in a similar domain, a similar organisation, or with the specific skills the role needs.
  [Non-technical overview]
- **N-7 Finance, accounting and regulated work:** name standards and systems with both the acronym
  and the full form ("Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP)", IFRS, QuickBooks, SAP).
  Recruiters filter on the software. [Resume job description]

## Bullets
- **N-8 Formula:** CAR (Challenge → Action → Result) or STAR, starting with an action verb, then the
  ad's keyword, then the result. The most relevant roles get 3–5 tailored bullets; cut or minimise
  irrelevant duties. [CAR guide; Resume job description; hybrid/Top 500+ Resume Keywords for 2026.md]
- **N-9 No "hard" metrics? There is still something measurable.** Use volume, frequency, speed,
  consistency, satisfaction, error reduction, output, time saved, scope, before/after, or a comparison
  ("double the volume in peak season"). Only real figures from the knowledge base. [CAR guide;
  Transferable skills]

## Structure and skills
- **N-10 Order:** Summary → Skills → most relevant experience → secondary roles → Education. Recent
  graduates put education first. [CAR guide; Sales resume]
- **N-11 Skills section:** 5–10 skills in the ad's exact phrasing (if the ad says Google Sheets, write
  Google Sheets, not just Excel), the critical ones first. No personality traits as skills
  ("hardworking", "motivated", "team player"); show those through results. No technical skills that
  don't serve the role. Test each skill: is it relevant, can the person prove it, has the CV shown
  why it matters? [CAR guide; Transferable skills]
- **N-12 Title:** match the posting's title where honest; it is the single highest-leverage change. If
  the person has never held it, the target title goes in the summary (C-3). [Resume job description]

## Form and context
- **N-13 Keep it simple:** no graphics, tables, columns or icons; leave white space; keep bullets
  concise. A little colour on headings is fine. [HR 5 tips; CAR guide]
- **N-14 Context the reader would otherwise guess:**
  - Be candid about gaps; a short reason beats leaving the reader to assume the worst.
  - Make location fit clear when the ad is location-bound; "remote in X" often means near X.
  - For career changers, C-14 applies in full, and the summary may carry a one-line objective.

  [Sales resume; HR 5 tips; Career change]
